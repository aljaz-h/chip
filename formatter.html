01Start from a preset

An AIOStreams formatter is two text templates: a name (the left column in a stream list) and a description (the lines under it). Pick a starting point, then change it. Everything runs in your browser.

    03Overlap with your badges

    Native badges and formatter text can show the same facts twice. Tick what your Chip badges already show, and this lists the template fields that repeat it. Remove them by hand, or start from the Badge-friendly preset.

      04Use it in AIOStreams

      1. Open your AIOStreams configuration and go to the Formatter section.
      2. Choose Custom, then paste the name and the description into their two boxes.
      3. Save, reinstall the addon if asked, and check a few real streams.

      Menu names can differ between versions. The preview is an approximation: your instance's version decides what really renders.
